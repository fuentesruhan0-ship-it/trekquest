TrekQuest: Smart Hiking Companion. A hiking app for Philippine trails that
works on or off the grid.

Core features:
- Home map with Satellite, Topo and Outdoor styles (Satellite is the default)
- Plan a Route: topo map, choose a starting point and destination anywhere
  on the map, auto-estimated hike time, saves to Upcoming (does not auto-start)
- Your Routes: Upcoming and History, with edit and delete
- Live hike tracking: route line, distance, speed, elevation, ETA, steps and
  calories from the device step sensor
- Reminders: water (every 30 min), rest and return time
- Offline plant scanner with species-level results and an offline Q&A assistant
- Weather, backpacking guide, first aid guide, survival manual
- Profile with emergency card and a Mountains Conquered gallery
- Privacy: no user is ever visible to other users on the map

Design: dark forest-green theme, coral-red primary buttons, frosted-glass panels.

Current state: pulled from Base44 into this folder. Known issues to fix first:

GPS, duplicate menu items (Emergency Card and Mountains Conquered are also
